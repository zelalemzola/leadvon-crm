What your accounts have been doing to your money
Here is the part nobody explained when you signed.
On a personal loan at 28% APR — the standard rate on most unsecured lending in South Africa — more than half of every minimum payment goes directly to interest. Not to the balance. To interest.
In the first two years of a typical loan, almost nothing you pay reduces what you owe. The balance barely moves. You've been paying faithfully, and the statement looks almost identical to last month. That is not because you're doing it wrong. That is because the minimum payment was calculated to work exactly that way.
The store accounts are worse. 
Jet. Mr Price. Truworths. The interest rates on those accounts sit at or near the legal maximum. A R2,000 balance with R150 minimum payments costs more than R9,000 over five years — and the balance is often still there.
This is not a side effect. It is the product design.
Every month you stay in the current structure, the majority of what leaves your salary goes to keeping the account open — not to closing it.

Three things worth knowing before the call
1. There are no upfront fees. Not now. Not ever.
 Registered debt counsellors are paid from within the restructured repayment plan — not before anything is done for you. If any person or company asks you for an upfront payment to start debt review, that is the clearest sign they are not registered. You can verify [AGENT FIRST NAME]'s registration at ncr.org.za before you do anything.
2. Your employer does not find out.
 Debt review is confidential. The National Credit Act does not require your employer to be notified. The only time your payroll department is involved is if an existing garnishee order is being modified — and that is an administrative instruction, not a complaint, not a disciplinary flag. Nothing else reaches your employer.
3. Your credit record recovers when the process ends.
 While debt review is active, a flag sits on your credit record that prevents new credit applications. When the process ends and your clearance certificate is issued, the flag is removed. A record showing paid accounts after debt review is materially better than a record accumulating defaults, missed payments, and judgements year after year. The person who completes debt review in four years has a recoverable credit record. The person who keeps managing as-is does not.
